# Perseus's Latin: duplication, not a gap-filler

[← back to README](../../README.md)

**Perseus's Latin is duplication, not a gap-filler**, and that's worth being
plain about. The archive holds 430 Latin texts but **no Augustine and no
Aquinas** — only Boethius among the patristic and medieval writers. So the
Latin doesn't fill the gap between antiquity and the Renaissance; it gives the
original of works whose translation is already there. That it's still worth
having is the same reason Runeberg and DTA are worth having at all: a question
about _pietas_, _virtus_ or _fortuna_ hits the words themselves rather than
nineteenth-century English paraphrases of them.
