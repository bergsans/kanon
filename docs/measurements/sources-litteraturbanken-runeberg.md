# Litteraturbanken and Projekt Runeberg

[← back to README](../../README.md)

**Litteraturbanken** has nearly the whole Swedish canon, but only some of the
editions are free: Strindberg's Collected Works and the Vitterhetssamfundet's
Almqvist sit there under licenses that explicitly forbid redistribution. The
generator only picks `cc-0`, and the fetch reads the rights line in every file.

**Projekt Runeberg** fills the gap it leaves — the old, free Strindberg editions
are there. Half the archive, however, is unread OCR of nineteenth-century print,
and such works are abandoned rather than indexed.
