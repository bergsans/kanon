### What can't be brought in

The protection term is the author's lifetime plus seventy years. Adorno
becomes free in 2040, Leo Strauss in 2044, Foucault in 2055, Cioran in 2066,
Lévi-Strauss in 2080 — and translations carry their own seventy years from the
translator's death. No database changes that, and the sites that have the
texts anyway have them without a license. The permalink also makes the app a
publisher: a saved search publishes a thousand characters of quotation at an
address.
