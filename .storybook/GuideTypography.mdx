# Typography

`--font-serif` and `--font-sans` in `globals.css` are OS system stacks today —
Iowan Old Style falling back to Georgia, `ui-sans-serif` falling back to the
platform default. Neither was chosen for how it looks, only for being
everywhere. This page weighs five replacement pairings against that baseline.
**None is adopted yet.**

The candidates lean toward the canon's own printing history rather than UI
fashion: a Garamond or Caslon revival for the serif, the lineage most of the
collection was actually set in, kept quiet by a restrained grotesque on the
sans side. Elegant here means fitting the subject.

## Diacritics

The interface is Swedish and the collection spans six languages. A face that
draws å, ß or œ poorly is out, however good its English looks.

## Trying another pairing

The fonts load through `next/font/google`, self-hosted at build time, so a new
candidate costs one entry in
[candidateFonts.ts](./illustrations/candidateFonts.ts) and nothing at runtime.
Adopting one means changing the two tokens in `@theme` in `globals.css`.
