# Spacing and surfaces

## The page shell

Every route shares one shell and one of three widths. The two search pages are
the only ones with two columns.

<PageShellWireframe />

## Breakpoints

The layout switches only at `sm` and `lg`, and Storybook's viewports are built
around those two. A third breakpoint needs a reason.

<BreakpointRuler />

## Responsive behaviour

The layout changes shape, not just size, at exactly two places:

<GuideTable>

| Component | `sm` (640px) | `lg` (1024px) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RecentSearches` | — | becomes a left-bordered column (`lg:border-l`) instead of a top-bordered block |
| `CorpusBrowser` | splits into a two-column grid (`sm:grid-cols-[13rem_minmax(0,1fr)]`) | — |
| `NavMenu` | trigger moves in from the edge (`sm:right-6 sm:top-6`), panel widens to `sm:w-80` | — |
| The page shell | — | splits into two columns (`lg:grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_15rem]`); the aside becomes `lg:sticky` |

</GuideTable>

Everything else that changes at `sm`/`lg` is a size — type scale, width —
not a shape.

## Shadows

Two tokens: `shadow-sheet` for a surface that rests on the page, `shadow-lift`
for one that floats above it, such as a menu, a dialog or the search box.

<ShadowSamples />

## Layers

Three `z-index` layers. Popovers sit at `z-20`, the menu trigger and its
backdrop at `z-40`, and anything that covers the page at `z-50`.

<ZIndexStack />

## Motion

Only where it explains a change of place or a wait:

<GuideTable>

| Animation | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `menu-slide-in`, `menu-backdrop-in` (0.2s) | the menu drawer and its backdrop |
| `animate-ping` | the dot that shows a search in progress |
| `animate-pulse` | loading skeletons |

</GuideTable>

<MotionSamples />

Every animation is switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. A new
animation isn't done until it is covered by that rule.
