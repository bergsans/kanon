# Parchment and ink

A reading room for old texts, not a dashboard. Five rules hold it together.

## 1. Paper and ink, nothing else

Five paper tones, four inks, one accent, checked against WCAG 2.1. No dark mode.

<ParchmentByRole />
<InkByRole />

Full breakdown: [Foundations/Color](?path=/docs/design-guide-foundations-color--docs).

## 2. Serif is the collection's voice, sans is the app's

Passage, quote, work title: serif. Button, label, metadata, year: sans. See
[Foundations/Color](?path=/docs/design-guide-foundations-color--docs).

## 3. Weight for a setting, fill for a narrowing

A setting (text size, language, sort order): weight and an underline. A
filter that narrows the question (genre, era): accent border and fill.

<SelectedStateSwatches />

The third column breaks the rule — see [Known issues](?path=/docs/design-guide-known-issues--docs).

## 4. The accent marks, it doesn't decorate

Red chalk: an active narrowing, a link, or the send button — never
"clickable" in general, that's what outlines and underlines are for. "All
subjects" is the rest state, so it's bold ink, not accent.

## 5. Flat and square

Every radius is zeroed in `@theme`; `rounded-full` survives only where shape
carries meaning — a pulse, a progress bar, a map pin, plus one icon button
(see [Known issues](?path=/docs/design-guide-known-issues--docs)). Shadows
are tinted with ink, never gray.
